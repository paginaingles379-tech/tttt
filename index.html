About Zacatlán

Zacatlán de las Manzanas is a Magical Town located in the mountains of Puebla, Mexico. It is well known for its apple production, cool climate, and beautiful landscapes.

The town combines history, gastronomy, traditions, and nature, making it one of the most charming destinations in central Mexico.
